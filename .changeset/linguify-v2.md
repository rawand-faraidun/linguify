Linguify 2: a rewrite. A command for every change to your translation files — `key add`, `set`, `rename` and `remove`, `namespace add`, `rename` and `remove`, `sync`, `diff`, `format`, `export` and `import` in csv, tsv, json or xlsx, `list` and `config` — each checked before anything is written and written whole, with `--dry-run`, `--json` and real exit codes. `linguify studio` is the translation table in your browser: flat, tree and compact views, search and filters, editing in place, on your machine only. Descriptions beside each key, single- or multi-file layouts, and a JSON schema for your editor. The config's names changed — see "Upgrading from v1" in the README.
